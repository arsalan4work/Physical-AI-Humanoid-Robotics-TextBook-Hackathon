# Welcome to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

This textbook is designed to provide a comprehensive and AI-native understanding of physical AI and humanoid robotics. Whether you're a beginner looking to grasp the fundamentals or an expert seeking in-depth implementation details, this book offers a structured learning path through a blend of theoretical concepts, practical code examples, and real-world applications.

## What You'll Learn

*   **ROS 2 Fundamentals**: The core operating system for robotics.
*   **Digital Twin Concepts**: Simulating robots in Gazebo and Unity.
*   **Vision-Language-Action Robotics**: Integrating AI with robot perception and action.
*   **Humanoid Kinematics**: Understanding the movement of humanoid robots.
*   **Hardware & Lab Architecture**: Setting up real-world robotics environments.

## How to Use This Textbook

This book is structured into modules, with each concept explained in three layers of depth:

1.  **Beginner**: Intuitive explanations and analogies.
2.  **Intermediate**: Core robotics and AI concepts.
3.  **Expert**: Detailed implementations, pipelines, and advanced examples.

Each chapter includes concept maps, diagrams, real-world examples, common mistakes, and exercises to reinforce your learning.

Let's begin our journey into the fascinating world of Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics!
